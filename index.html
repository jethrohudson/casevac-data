CASEVAC: Hospital data

Public hospital, trauma-center, stroke-center and heliport data used by the CASEVAC: Nearest Trauma Center iPhone app to update its offline database without an app update.

  • data/v1/manifest.json (version, dates, SHA-256 and size of each file)
  • data/v1/hospitals.json
  • data/v1/heliports.json
  • data/v1/dataset_meta.json (sources, licenses, coverage)

Sources: HIFLD Open Hospitals (public domain), CMS Hospital General Information (U.S. Government work), FAA NASR (public domain), CDC stroke-center certifications (U.S. Government work), OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0).

Not for emergency use on its own. In an emergency, call 911. Terms and privacy: jethrohudson.github.io/casevac-legal
